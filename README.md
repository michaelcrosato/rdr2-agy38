## 🤠 Overview

Set in May 1899 on the American Frontier, **DEAD HORIZON: 1899** chronicles the tragic, beautiful decline of the outlaw era. Players step into the worn leather boots of **Silas Vance**, senior enforcer and moral compass of the **Sterling Gang**, led by the silver-tongued visionary **Julian 'The Prophet' Sterling** and veteran mentor **Josiah Finch**.

As iron tracks, electric wires, industrial billionaires, and federal detective agencies close the net, Silas must navigate loyalty, greed, and a fatal illness to secure the future of his younger brother-in-arms **Jack Caldwell**, his wife **Alma**, and young **Jack Jr**.

## 🌟 Key Features & Systems

### 1. Complete 153-Mission Campaign & Compendium
Every single story beat, battle, robbery, companion activity, and stranger strand from Red Dead Redemption 2 is recreated with original Western narratives, dialogue, objectives, and gold medal challenges:
- **Chapter 1: Colter Ridge (Snow & Survival)** — 6 missions (Blizzard survival, train heist, saving Sarah Cross).
- **Chapter 2: Buffalo Springs (The Frontier Town)** — 22 missions (Saloon brawl, sheep rustling, Thomas Downes debt, oil tanker heist, Valentine bank robbery).
- **Chapter 3: Scarlett Pines (Feuding Dynasties)** — 18 missions (The Blackwoods vs Galloways, moonshine stills, tobacco field inferno, assault on Blackwood Manor).
- **Chapter 4: New Bordeaux (Gilded City & Shadows)** — 17 missions (Trolley car robbery, riverboat high-stakes poker, Don Cesare's palace, Saint Denis bank disaster).
- **Chapter 5: Isla de Fuego (The Storm & Rebellion)** — 10 missions (Caribbean shipwreck, gallows rebellion, fortress cannon defense, the long acoustic ride home).
- **Chapter 6: Iron Ridge (Redemption & Sunrise)** — 25 missions (Fort Wallace rescue, Bacchus bridge dynamite, Downes family absolution, Silas Vance's high-honor cliffside sunrise finale).
- **Epilogue Part 1: Red Rock Ranch (Building a Life)** — 11 missions (Jack Caldwell farm labor, defending the ranch, buying Whispering Pines).
- **Epilogue Part 2: Whispering Pines (A New Jerusalem)** — 13 missions (House-building hammer montage, proposal on the mountain lake, the final summit shootout on Mount Hagen avenging Silas Vance).
- **All 31 Stranger Side Missions** — The Veteran & Buell War Horse, The Wildlife Photographer, Nikola Vance's Mad Inventions, 4 Legendary Gunslinger Duels, Serial Killer Basement Clue Hunt, Dinosaur Bones, Time Traveler Carvings, Legendary Catfish, and more.

### 2. Dead Eye Targeting & Bullet Time
- **Time Dilation**: World time decelerates to 0.2× speed.
- **Visual Amber Filter**: Sepia/amber vignette wash across the screen.
- **Target Painting**: Paint red 'X' marks on lethal enemy hitboxes (head, heart, limbs).
- **Execution Barrage**: Lightning-fast hammer-fanning firing sequence discharging at all painted coordinates with bespoke smoke and ricochet sounds.

### 3. Procedural 3D Western Horse & 4-Tier Bonding
- Custom articulated quadruped rig (`WesternHorse`) with realistic trot, canter, gallop, rear, and grazing IK animations.
- Procedural leather western saddle with horn, Navajo blanket, stirrups, flowing tail, and reins.
- **4 Bonding Levels**:
  - Level 1: Basic riding and hitching.
  - Level 2: Whistle call range +100m, manual rearing (`Space`).
  - Level 3: Skid stops and drift slides.
  - Level 4: Piaffe dressage step, -50% stamina drain reduction.
- Horse grooming, brushing, feeding (carrots, apples, oatcakes), and saddle weapon storage.

### 4. RDR2 Cores & Outer Bars System
- **Health Core (Inner Heart)** + Health Outer Ring.
- **Stamina Core (Inner Lightning Bolt)** + Stamina Outer Ring.
- **Dead Eye Core (Inner Eye)** + Dead Eye Outer Ring.
- Core depletion from hunger/cold; replenished by Pearson's camp stew, provisions, and miracle tonics.

### 5. Frontier Ballistics & Weapon Customization
- Full arsenal: Cattleman Revolver, Schofield, Volcanic, Lancaster Repeater, Springfield Rifle, Bolt-Action Rifle, Double-Barreled Shotgun, Hunting Bow, Dynamite, Fire Bottles, Lasso, and Hunting Knife.
- Dynamic weapon condition degradation (carbon fouling) restored using **Gun Oil**.
- Saloon brawling combos (jab, cross, uppercut, grapple, throw into furniture).

### 6. Dynamic Law & Morality Honor System
- **Witness Mechanics**: Civilians alert deputies with real-time fleeing eye markers.
- **Wanted Radius**: Lawmen cordon zones and pursue on horseback.
- **Territory Bounties**: Pay off bounties via Post Office telegraph stations.
- **Morality Honor Meter (-100 to +100)**: Influences store prices (up to 50% discount), civilian interactions, and story endings.

### 7. Hunting, Skinning & Interactive Fishing
- Roaming wildlife herds (Grizzly Bears, Timber Wolves, Whitetail Bucks, Bisons, Alligators, Cougars).
- **Eagle Eye**: Golden shimmering scent trails tracking animal paths and wild herbs.
- **3-Star Clean Kill System**: Pristine pelts awarded only for headshots using correct caliber/bow.
- **Interactive Fishing**: Rod casting, nibble detection, hook timing, and reel tension gauge.

### 8. Living Gang Camp & Saloon Mini-Games
- Gang camp with Julian, Finch, Sadie, Jack, Alma, and Cookie Potts.
- Camp chores: chop firewood, haul water buckets (+Honor & +Dead Eye XP).
- Camp donation ledger and wagon upgrades (medicine, provisions, ammo, fast travel map).
- Daily camp stew pot granting fortified golden cores.
- **Texas Hold'em Poker**: Interactive saloon card game against AI outlaws with chip betting, raises, and showdowns.
- **Five Finger Fillet**: Fast-paced knife rhythm sequence between fingers.

### 9. Silas Vance's Hand-Drawn Journal
- Leatherbound journal featuring hand-drawn sketches (mountains, stags, skulls) and Silas's personal reflections on missions and mortality.

### 10. Procedural Western Synth Score
- Dynamic soundtrack: Campfire acoustic guitar, "High Noon Shootout" galloping percussion, Ennio Morricone whistler motifs, and melancholic choir melodies.

## 🛠 Technology & Architecture

- **Engine Foundation**: `my-3d2dge-agent.js` (custom extended with `WesternHorse`, `Stagecoach`, `TrainLocomotive`, and Stetson/Bowler hat rigs).
- **Module Architecture**:
  - `src/data/missions.js` — All 153 canonical missions.
  - `src/data/compendium.js` — Weapons, fauna, fish, horses, provisions, camp ledger.
  - `src/systems/world.js` — Multi-region tile map, train route, day/night lighting.
  - `src/systems/deadeye.js` — Time dilation, target painting, execution barrage.
  - `src/systems/horse.js` — Quadruped movement, mounting, 4-tier bonding.
  - `src/systems/combat.js` — Ballistics, weapon condition, cover, brawling, enemy AI.
  - `src/systems/law.js` — Wanted levels, witnesses, territory bounties, honor meter.
  - `src/systems/hunting.js` — Wildlife AI, Eagle Eye scent trails, pelt grading, fishing.
  - `src/systems/camp.js` — Living gang camp, chores, stew pot, donation ledger.
  - `src/systems/minigames.js` — Texas Hold'em Poker, Five Finger Fillet.
  - `src/systems/journal.js` — Leatherbound journal with sketches and reflections.
  - `src/systems/mission-runner.js` — Campaign progression & 153-quest browser.
  - `src/systems/audio.js` — Procedural Web Audio API Western synthesizer score.
  - `src/game.js` — Main game loop, camera focus, and RDR2 Cores HUD.
  - `index.html` — Responsive host with retro CRT scanline shader and mobile touch clusters.
